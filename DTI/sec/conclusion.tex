\section{Conclusion}

We presented TAMR-DTI, a multimodal DTI framework centered on target-aware modulation and Mamba-based protein representation refinement. In five repeated runs across four DTI benchmarks, TAMR-DTI improves the reported AUROC/AUPRC on BindingDB, BioSNAP, and C. elegans, while Human remains a clear limitation. These results support TAMR-DTI as a competitive and dataset-dependent model, with further component-level validation needed to isolate the contribution of each proposed component.
