\section{Discussion}

The current evidence supports a conservative interpretation. In the main five-seed experiments, TAMR-DTI improves ranking metrics on three of four datasets but remains below the reported reference result on Human. The results do not support a claim of uniform dominance across all benchmarks. Instead, they suggest that target-aware conformer aggregation and protein-side state-space refinement can improve ranking quality when the dataset distribution benefits from pair-specific modulation, while thresholded metrics remain sensitive to validation-threshold selection and class distribution.
