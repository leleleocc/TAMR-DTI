\begin{abstract}
Drug-target interaction (DTI) prediction requires models that can jointly exploit molecular topology, molecular geometry, protein sequence context, and cross-modal drug-target matching. We propose TAMR-DTI, a multimodal DTI framework that combines target-aware modulation and Mamba-based representation refinement. The modulation component lets protein context guide drug conformer aggregation while ligand information conditions protein token encoding. The refinement component applies state-space sequence modeling only to protein tokens, preserving BiIntention as the explicit cross-modal matching module. Across five repeated runs on four benchmark datasets, TAMR-DTI improves the reported reference AUROC/AUPRC on BindingDB, BioSNAP, and C. elegans, with the largest gains on BioSNAP. On Human, TAMR-DTI remains below the reported reference result, indicating that the benefit of target-aware modulation and protein-side Mamba refinement is dataset-dependent rather than uniformly dominant.
\end{abstract}
